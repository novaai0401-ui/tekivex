Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Pyntra ("we", "us") is a free tool for reading, editing and protecting documents that runs in your web browser at pyntra.tekivex.com. This policy explains what data is and isn't handled when you use it.

1. Your files stay on your device

Opening, editing, converting, OCR, redaction, the Blackout game and the catch finder all run locally in your browser. Your documents are never uploaded to our servers. Work you save is stored in your browser's local storage / IndexedDB on your own device so it can reopen automatically; you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

2. Analytics

The marketing page at /welcome uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage, such as how many people visit and from which kinds of device. Google Analytics sets cookies and may collect a truncated IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited. It never receives your files. The app itself does not load Google Analytics unless the site operator turns it on. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics Opt-out.

3. Blackout retention counts

To learn whether the daily case is worth keeping, your browser sends one small, anonymous message when you finish a daily case. It contains only two things: the week you first played (the Monday's date) and how many days have passed since then. It contains no identifier, no cookie, no score, no case content and nothing from your documents. Our server keeps only running totals per week and day; it does not store IP addresses. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is sent. Practice cases and games on your own files send nothing.

4. No advertising

Pyntra does not show ads and does not use advertising cookies.

5. Optional AI features

AI features are optional. When you use one, only the text needed for it leaves your browser:

  • Your own AI provider. If you configure an AI provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq) in settings, the question you ask and the document text it needs go directly from your browser to the endpoint you configured, using your key. This is off by default.
  • Pyntra's AI service. Where the site operator has enabled it, your question and the document text it needs go to our server, which forwards them to Groq's API and returns the answer. We do not store prompts or responses.
  • "Ask AI for more" in the catch finder sends the document's text to whichever of the above you have set up. It is hidden when neither is configured.

Please review the privacy policy of any AI provider you use.

6. Real-time collaboration

If you share a document for real-time co-editing, edits are relayed between participants through a relay server. These frames are end-to-end encrypted: the relay only forwards already-encrypted data and cannot read your content. Collaboration is only active when you create or join a share link.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage / IndexedDB to autosave your work, remember preferences and keep your Blackout streak on your device, and analytics cookies on /welcome (Section 2). You can delete them through your browser settings; the editing tools keep working without them.

8. Children's privacy

Pyntra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your choices & rights

Because your files never leave your device, you remain in control of your own content. For analytics data handled by Google, you can use the opt-out link above. Depending on your location (e.g. the EEA, UK, or California) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you; contact us to exercise them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
