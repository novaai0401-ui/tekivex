Questions

Is Pyntra free?

Yes. No account, no ads and no watermark on your output.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Files are processed in your browser and stay on your device. Real-time collaboration is end-to-end encrypted.

Can I play Blackout on my own document?

Yes. Open the file, then choose Smart Tools, Read, Play. Each passage hides its most specific words.

Can I edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint?

Yes. They open in their own editors, and you can convert to and from PDF.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install it as an app and it keeps working without a connection.

Is the catch finder legal advice?

No. It points out common clause patterns worth a second look and quotes the sentence so you can judge it.
