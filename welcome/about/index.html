About Pyntra

Pyntra is a free, privacy-first document tool that runs entirely in your web browser. There is no account to create and nothing to install (though you can add it to your home screen as an app). Most importantly, your files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to a server — which makes Pyntra safe for sensitive, personal and confidential material.

What you can do with Pyntra

  • Read — play Blackout on today's case or your own file, find the catch in a contract, ask questions with page citations, OCR scans, and listen with read aloud.
  • Fix — edit text in any PDF, fill and sign forms, reorder and merge pages, and edit Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files in their own editors.
  • Hide — truly redact text so it is gone from the file, scan for personal data, and password-protect PDFs.
  • Prove — check that redactions hold, audit accessibility, and compare two versions.

Our approach to privacy

Pyntra is designed so that the powerful stuff — editing, OCR, true redaction, conversion and the catch finder — happens locally in the browser. That means faster results, offline capability, and documents that stay yours. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Who it's for

Students, families, small businesses, creators and professionals who want capable tools without paywalls, watermarks, sign-ups, or uploading private files to a stranger's server.
